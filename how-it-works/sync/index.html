Push wakes. Pull syncs.

Between servers or to your client, the pattern is the same: notify, pull, catch up.

Servers and clients share a sync pattern: notify, pull, catch up.

01

One pattern, two connections

Peer servers and clients both pull updates after a wake-up hint. Their transports, authentication and APIs need not be identical.

02

Hints are not message data

The receiving server or client requests updates and saves its progress as it processes the returned data.

03

Reconnect and catch up

Both can retrieve missed updates from saved positions. The client example illustrates reconnecting; batching and retries are simplified.
