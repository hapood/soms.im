Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about self-hosting, federation, and SOMS architecture.

What is SOMS designed for?

SOMS brings self-hosted messaging and native federation together. Run an independent server, use email-like addresses, and exchange direct messages and group conversations across domains while keeping message history on your server.

How does SOMFP cross-domain federation work?

Accounts use email-like addresses such as alice@soms.im. To reach another domain, servers discover and recognize each other, then exchange signed requests over HTTPS. This protects server-to-server transport; it is not user-to-user end-to-end encryption.

Which clients can I use?

Use SOMS on the web, on desktop, or with the Android client. Clients support direct messages, group conversations, media sharing, and synchronized message history. Android push notifications help you keep up with new messages on the go.

What should I plan for when self-hosting?

Follow the deployment guide for the current installation steps and database configuration. Plan for HTTPS, persistent storage, and backups of your database, uploaded files, and configuration. Resource needs depend on your workload; test with your expected usage before choosing server capacity.
