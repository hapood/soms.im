Vision & Architecture

Rethinking messaging for the self-hosted era.

Email-like cross-domain simplicity, featherweight resource footprint, full ownership of your chat assets, and a foundation for lightweight AI agents.

Core Philosophy

Designed from the ground up for personal servers, homelabs, and true sovereignty.

🌐

Federation as Simple as Email

Just enter alice@example.com to connect with friends or join multi-server group chats across independent domains. No central directory, no shared database. Server traffic runs over standard HTTPS with Ed25519 request signatures for transparent, debuggable federation.

⚡

Featherweight Footprint (~160–200MB)

Engineered to run as a single clean stack (Fastify + Drizzle ORM + PostgreSQL) with zero external message brokers (no Redis, Kafka, or RabbitMQ). Durable pull sync automatically catches up on all missed messages when a residential server reboots or reconnects.

🗄️

Own Your History & Assets

Your conversations and files are long-term personal digital assets. Storing history cleanly in standard PostgreSQL gives you instant server-side full-text search, effortless multi-device sync, and reliable backups without complex key-recovery rituals.

The Architectural Trade-Off

Why SOMS avoids heavy federation-layer E2EE in favor of self-hosted data ownership.

S2S Transport Security vs. Fragile Key-Gossip Blobs

Many modern decentralized protocols enforce end-to-end encryption across all federated participants. While well-intentioned, this frequently turns personal chat history into undecryptable binary blobs whenever keys desync, prevents fast server-side searching, drains mobile device batteries, and makes automated personal backups exceedingly difficult.

SOMS takes a pragmatic, email-inspired stance: Server-to-server traffic is strictly authenticated and encrypted via TLS and Ed25519 signatures, but message storage remains clean, relational, and directly queryable on your personal node. You maintain absolute physical and cryptographic ownership of your server disk without sacrificing usability.

Project Roadmap

Current progress and upcoming milestones for the SOMS ecosystem.

Core Protocol & Durable Sync

Delivered

SOMFP protocol specification draft, Ed25519 request signing, cursor-based pull sync for reliable packet recovery, and tombstone propagation for clean cross-server revocations.

Multi-Platform Native Clients

Delivered

Lean Web/PWA, Desktop (Rust/Tauri ~34MB), and Android Alpha clients. Includes WebRTC 1:1 voice/video calling, background push notifications, and unified multi-device read states.

UX Polish & One-Click NAS Stacks

In Progress

Refining client responsiveness and expanding our interactive Docker Compose generator with plug-and-play templates for Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS, and residential homelab environments.

Lightweight AI Agents & Workflows

Planned

Native webhook bridges and agent adapters. Because message records are structured and self-hosted, SOMS will serve as a dynamic workspace where humans and lightweight AI agents collaborate seamlessly.
